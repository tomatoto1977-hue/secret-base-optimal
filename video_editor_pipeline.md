# Secret Base Video Editor Pipeline

## Policy
The production path does not require paid or metered video SaaS.

## Roles
1. Secret Base: script, original visuals, narration, BGM/SFX, captions, 1080x1920 H.264/AAC master.
2. DaVinci Resolve 21: primary human finishing environment for edit, color, audio, motion graphics, captions and final export.
3. AviUtl ExEdit2: optional specialist layer for 2D motion and detailed effects.
4. Shotcut: free fallback for lightweight editing, conversion and recovery.

## Required handoff
- 9:16 / 1080x1920
- 30 fps
- MP4 / H.264 / AAC
- human approval before external publication
- no paid SaaS is required

## Safety
No automatic publishing, money operations, credential handling, or external account actions are part of the editor handoff.
